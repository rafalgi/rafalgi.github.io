About me

I am a Python engineer focused on Generative AI: LLM applications, RAG pipelines and agentic workflows taken from prototype to production. At co.brick I am the sole developer of an enterprise AI platform running on Google Cloud, and in parallel I do RAG research at the Institute of Theoretical and Applied Informatics, Polish Academy of Sciences (IITiS PAN).

My research is about information retrieval and RAG optimisation: chunking strategies, embedding and retrieval hyperparameters, pipeline evaluation, and lately also LLM agents that compress neural networks for edge devices. In 2026 I co-authored three peer-reviewed workshop papers at ACM CIKM and ECML PKDD.

I study Computer Science at the Faculty of Applied Mathematics, Silesian University of Technology. Day to day I work with LangChain, LangGraph, Google ADK, MCP, vector databases and AI-assisted development tools.

Apart from AI, I train judo and I run.
