About me

I am an AI Python Developer at co.brick, a company building an AI platform for industry, and an MSc student in Computer Science (Machine Learning specialization) at the Faculty of Applied Mathematics, Silesian University of Technology.

My work sits between applied engineering and research. I am mostly interested in Retrieval-Augmented Generation: how to evaluate RAG pipelines, how to choose their hyperparameters without exhaustive search, and how to make retrieval reliable in real, messy industrial data. I also work on efficient ML, using LLMs to guide the optimization of neural networks for edge devices.

I contribute to the PACK Horizon Europe research project. In 2026 I co-authored three workshop papers: two at ECML PKDD 2026 (Naples) and one, as first author, at CIKM 2026 (Rome).
